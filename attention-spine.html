Is your agent behaving properly?

Correct-looking behavior is not always correct

An agent can return a plausible answer while the judge scoring it has stopped discriminating, a dimension it depends on goes unobserved, or an interpreted value reaches a decision point without the evidence to justify it. The attention spine reads the record for these conditions: judge behavior, evaluation structure, coverage and reach, surface lifecycle, seams and authority, and the integrity of the store itself. Every finding source reports into one queue, ranked by what needs attention, with its evidence attached.

The Spine Alarms

These alerts fire whenever Holonograph maps a discrepancy in any of these categories. Alarms stack across surfaces and cohorts, can be triaged, and eventually phase out if transient. Maintenance of these alarms will reduce them until the surface’s full authority is granted.

Curate and approve

A confirmed finding becomes a corrective proposal. A person investigates and approves it; the change then enters the record like any other.

The short descriptions are plain-language readings of each alarm's name, not specifications of its detector.

From an alarm to a corrective change

Attribution depends on setup. Surfaces are declared, set up orthogonally, variance controlled, and cohort tagged with relevant substrate columns. Each alarm then arrives with its attributed source: what moved with it, and by how much.

Example alarms

  • Agent change: tool_discipline -3.8 pts. Where it points: Your agent. The drop starts at prompt v15, which cut the tool-use rule. Correction: Revert to prompt v14. Restores the tool-use rule.
  • Model change: analysis_quality_judged -5.2 pts. Where it points: The model. The vendor moved your alias from model 4.1 to 4.2. Your repo did not change. Correction: Pin back to model 4.1. Adjust the prompt for 4.2, test it on a cohort, then upgrade.
  • Eval change: hypothesis_grounding +2.9 pts. Where it points: Your eval. The score rose at rubric v5. The agent did not change. Correction: Rebaseline on rubric v5. The rise is a scoring change. The trend restarts at v5.
  • Visibility gap: title_quality -2.2 pts. Where it points: Visibility. Prompt v9 and model 4.2 shipped together. No substrate column separates them. Correction: Hold a cohort on model 4.1. Cohort tag it. The next runs separate the two axes.
