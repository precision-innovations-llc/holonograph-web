About

Built by the person who needed it first.

Holonograph is built by Precision Innovations. It began with a question every operator of an agentic system runs into: did this change fix the problem, and did it quietly cause a new one?

The origin

Holonograph started with a practical need, not a philosophy. Running a real agentic system in production, one question kept coming back: when you change something, did the change fix the problem, and did it quietly cause a new one? Answering that honestly means attributing what changed to its real source. The formal version of that argument is written up in The Potentiality Field.

The honesty came second. Holonograph can attribute a change in full some of the time, in part other times, and not at all in others. What makes it an instrument rather than a guess is that it knows which case it is in, and says so. An honest account of what it cannot resolve is the byproduct of measuring what it can.

The company

Precision Innovations LLC builds precision-engineered software and hardware, designed with rigor and shipped with care. The through-line is a standard rather than a category: scientific rigor applied to the product outcome, not only the build. Holonograph is its flagship.

That standard becomes two commitments Holonograph makes. Sovereignty: it runs in your infrastructure and emits no telemetry, so your prompts, traffic, and results never leave your perimeter. Honesty: it reports its own floor, estimates the size of its blind spots, and will call a shift noise rather than dress it up as a cause. The four-source attribution method is patent pending.
