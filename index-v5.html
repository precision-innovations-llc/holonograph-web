The problem

Software can be correct about the wrong thing.

  1. Parserextracts a value
  2. Matcherresolves an identity
  3. Classifierselects a rule
  4. Agentinterprets an instruction

Every handoff carries an interpretation into the next decision. That handoff is a seam: a location where an interpretation becomes consequential.

Software may behave exactly as implemented while acting on an interpretation that was incomplete, incorrect, or never qualified for the decision it influenced.

Tests can confirm the implementation without establishing the authority of its interpretive inputs.

No decision surface starts with authority. Every one of them establishes it through observation, and evidence that held yesterday can stop holding after a relevant change. A regex-derived candidate set may be treated as the complete set of applicable rules without evidence that the selection was complete.

Agentic systems compound this problem, at machine speed. These incomplete or inaccurate parses exist as ground truths to model interpretation. Models introduce their own stochastic behavior, external dependencies, and changing evaluation behavior. Coding agents accelerate the creation and modification of the software surrounding those models.

Consequential decisions need an observable basis for authority.

Holonograph identifies where interpretation influences those decisions and provides the measurement infrastructure to determine what the available evidence supports.

AI-driven decisions depend on more than LLM outputs.

Application logic, data transformations, heuristics, tools, and LLM interpretations can all influence consequential outcomes. Holonograph identifies those dependencies and observes their behavior as the surrounding system evolves.

A seam is not necessarily a defect.

It is a location where an interpretation becomes consequential.

The purpose of discovering it is not automatically to remove it. It is to make its provenance, permitted influence, and subsequent behavior observable.

The instrument

Attribution at the source of change.

Observability alone does not establish attribution. Holonograph separates four sources of variance within an immutable event.

SourceWhat it represents
SubstrateThe evaluated system, its inputs, tools, code, and declared operating context
Light sourceThe model and provider-controlled behavior
LensThe evaluation apparatus, including judges, criteria, and configurations
Stochastic floorResidual variation characterized by the measurement procedure

A change in the measurement apparatus can resemble a change in the system being measured. Holonograph preserves the identities and relationships needed to investigate that difference.

Research-grade measurement

The Lens supports versioned evaluation surfaces, structured facets and subfacets, judge-model attribution, rubric management, saturation diagnostics, orthogonality analysis, dark-surface discovery, non-primary model multiplexing, and longitudinal cohort monitoring.

Judge behavior is itself observable. Evaluation criteria can change, discrimination can weaken, and apparent stability can conceal a saturated measurement channel.

The instrument records those conditions rather than treating a judge's score as self-validating evidence.

The lifecycle

Establish the observation surface.

Define the observational environment, immutable evidence, and facet architecture.

These operations establish what the instrument is observing and what evidence the surface is expected to provide.

They are not hand-run. Dark-surface analysis reports traffic arriving with no declared surface, so the map extends from observed behavior rather than from a human inventory. The SDK lets a coding agent read and revise surface contracts directly in the repository, against the operator's stated preferences. Attributional schemas are declared as versioned code and applied, the way infrastructure is.

Discover

Identify consequential interpretation boundaries in code, agent workflows, model interactions, and runtime behavior.

Source analysis locates candidate decision points. Runtime observation identifies activity associated with declared and undeclared surfaces.

Declare

Define the surface's identity, expected behavior, relevant dimensions, and authority requirements.

A declaration describes what must be measured. It does not establish that the requirements have been satisfied.

Measure

Collect attributable observations against the declared contract.

Use deterministic checks, structured evaluation, LLM judges, or appropriate combinations. Qualify the measurement channel before relying on its results.

Outcome

An identifiable, instrumented surface with explicit measurement requirements. This stage can often be completed during initial integration; for a bounded surface, setup may fit into an afternoon. It does not automatically confer operational authority.

Attribute. Monitor. Requalify.

Authority requires continuing evidence.

A declared surface does not automatically earn authority. Its observations must establish the required conditions, and subsequent changes may invalidate that evidence.

Attribute

Investigate behavioral changes using the available substrate, light-source, Lens, and stochastic evidence.

Distinguish changes in the evaluated system from changes in the measurement apparatus where the observations permit that distinction.

Monitor

Observe longitudinal behavior through declared cohorts, facets, transfer characteristics, saturation diagnostics, and attention-spine alarms.

Track which surfaces remain within their established measurement and operating conditions.

Requalify

When relevant conditions change, evaluate whether the existing evidence remains sufficient.

Authority may remain established, become withdrawn, or require additional observation. Requalification follows the surface's declared requirements rather than a universal waiting period.

Outcome

An evolving record of what each surface has established, what remains unresolved, and what evidence is required next.

The principle

Intelligence is inexpensive. Authority must be earned.

Agentic systems can generate interpretations, decisions, and software changes at increasing speed. The evidence required to justify consequential behavior does not necessarily accumulate at that speed.

A new decision surface may be discovered and instrumented in an afternoon. Establishing its behavior under relevant operating conditions may require substantially more observation. And evidence that was sufficient yesterday may no longer be sufficient after a relevant change.

Holonograph maintains the record between those moments.

This is the commercial meaning of longitudinal authority governance. It does not promise permanent validation. It provides an explicit account of what has been established, what has changed, and what remains to be demonstrated.

Engineering record

Authority-seam analysis on a rule-based calendaring agent

During development of a rule-based calendaring agent, authority-seam analysis identified consequential dependencies that a separate rule audit had missed.

A heuristic filer-side inference was presented as established information and influenced which rules reached the model.

A fuzzy matter match received an authoritative label that did not distinguish it from an exact identifier match.

Subsequent investigation identified additional authority-bearing dependencies in rule selection and evaluation context.

These findings demonstrate the practical importance of examining how interpreted values influence downstream decisions, including decisions made before a model call.

The seam reports required investigation and disposition; a reported seam is not automatically a defect.

Research

What Does an LLM Judge Actually Measure?

Two prospectively governed studies examined whether evaluation frames established discrimination of a target construct.

Across 13,440 canonical judgments, the studies showed that the registered inferential status of target discrimination depended on the evaluation frame.

The target-bound measurement channel was established across three evaluator configurations. The stronger preregistered binding-recovery claim was not supported, and conditional attenuation remained unidentified.

The work illustrates why measurement-channel qualification must precede consequential scientific inference.

Evidence boundary

Holonograph reports what the declared measurement contract establishes. Its record does not independently certify the correctness of a consequential decision beyond that contract and its qualified evidence.

Public demonstrations, engineering records, and controlled studies establish different things. Engineering findings demonstrate observed utility in specific workflows. Controlled studies support their registered scientific conclusions.

Neither establishes universal scanner coverage, permanent correctness, or guaranteed operational outcomes.
