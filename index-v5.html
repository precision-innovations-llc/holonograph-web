Authority governance

A statistical attribution matrix for interpreted decisions

Interpreted data runs through valid code, reaches a decision point, and is elevated from inference to assumed truth. That assumption can propagate through downstream decisions, compounding unnoticed until the software produces a wrong result, or the right result for the wrong reason.

Holonograph identifies interpretive dependencies at machine speed, shows where they influence consequential decisions, and grants authority only while the evidence holds.

The problem

A valid value can carry a wrong interpretation

Conceptual example

A ticket reads “I hope your launch is a success.” A pattern pulls out success, the system files it as a resolved incident, and closes it.

Every check passes. The interpretation is wrong, and the test suite cannot see it, because the test suite checks the code.

The interpreter changes from system to system: a pattern over free text, a fuzzy match on a name, a classifier choosing a rule, a model answering a question. The handoff does not. Once the value is a validated fact, every decision after it inherits the assumption.

Authority governance

Authority, derived from the record

Authority is the state, held per decision surface, that the declared prerequisites for a consequential transition are satisfied on the record as it stands. It is granted on evidence, re-derived at every read, and no actor confers it. It sits on top of the attention spine and reads the same record.

What authority means

PermissionAn actor is allowed to act.
ApprovalAn actor affirmatively accepts something.
CorrectnessA proposition matches an applicable reference.
ConfidenceA degree of belief, or a measurement's precision.
AuthorityThe declared prerequisites for this transition are satisfied on the record.

Humans, builders and automated sources all produce events on the record. An operator approving a decision is approval, recorded as such. It does not move the state.

Six states

unmapped

No decision here is known to act on an interpreted value yet.

mapped

A decision is identified. Nothing asserts authority over it.

declared

Authority is asserted, but no qualifying evidence is accruing.

qualifying

Live traffic is accumulating the evidence.

granted

The condition is established and every dependency holds.

suspended

A dependency left its declared envelope. The grant is withdrawn.

The first five are a ladder. The sixth is a withdrawal branch, never a completion stage. Re-establishing a grant takes the re-satisfaction of every gate over the post-change record, not an acknowledgement.

Two things never confer authority.

An approved corrective change is a recorded change. It enters the record; it does not feed a grant. Whether delivery proceeds is operator or CI policy reading the record; the lens never blocks on its own authority.

Reflection, once a judge certifies

When a response fails a certified judge, the judge's grounded critique can go back to the producer for a capped retry. Holonograph grants that per judge, rubric, facet and surface, only after the cell certifies, and revokes it on any change to the identity it was computed on.

Reflection against an uncertified judge turns the judge into an optimization target, and the cheapest way for a producer to raise a score on a requires-confirmation dimension is to stop being honest about what it does not know. So the loop opens only where authority already stands.

Seams

Where inference becomes a decision

An interpreted value driving a decision that governs a consequential transition is a seam. It is permanent. You can close it and you can monitor it. You cannot validate it away.

Provenance survives every hop

A validation boundary filters the catastrophic and passes the shape-valid-but-wrong. It raises the floor and, in the same motion, makes the remaining error harder to see: the value now looks right, types right, and fails downstream. Moving the decision earlier only moves the seam; the next scan finds it in its new place.

Closed

The decision has a defined path for inputs it does not recognize, and everything downstream either decides on the same terms or does not decide at all. Ready to observe.

Open

Somewhere in the chain a decision has no answer for what it does not recognize, and the gap falls through into a consequence. The defective state.

A model is a seam whose interpreter moves

A pattern does not rewrite itself between releases. A model can be re-routed behind an unchanged name, and the instrument scoring it is usually another model. So a model seam carries more variance control. The lifecycle is the same.

Attribution

When a score moves, investigate what moved with it

A changed score can reflect your deployment, the model that answered, the evaluation, or noise. Holonograph keeps those identities on every observation and reads each declared dimension against all four, across every version. Where the record cannot separate two explanations, the reading says so and names the observation that would.

The attribution matrix

Attribution requires memory

A number can move without the behavior moving. When the lens that scores a surface is revised, the metric drifts while the behavior may not have changed at all. Holonograph versions every lens and keeps the full history, so each reading is checked against the exact lens that produced it.

The model you asked for and the model that answered are two different facts. A vendor can re-route behind an unchanged alias. Holonograph records both sides, so that change has a name instead of showing up as unexplained drift. The judge has the same two sides.

Research

What Does an LLM Judge Actually Measure?

The Role of Evaluation Frames in Construct Discrimination. Brian Kerrigan, 2026.

The finding

An LLM judge can produce a coherent rationale and a stable-looking score without establishing that the score measures the defect the operator intended. Two prospectively governed studies tested controlled defects across three fixed evaluator configurations: 4,800 judgments under ordinary holistic scoring, then 8,640 across three evaluation frames.

Under ordinary holistic scoring, no configuration established the preregistered target-discrimination criterion. Under target-bound scoring, all three did. Before relying on a judge for consequential inference, its ability to discriminate the relevant construct should be shown under the intended evaluation frame.

Calls passed through dedicated local Holonograph Lens instances, which supplied call-time correlation, route and served-model identity, substrate stamps, cohort tags, and immutable digest-only events. Raw requests and responses stayed outside the Lens.

Disclosure. Holonograph is developed by the study's author. The manuscript states this in its own methods, and so does this page.
