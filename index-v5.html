Authority governance

Longitudinal attribution for interpreted decisions.

Interpreted data runs through valid code, reaches a decision point, and is elevated from inference to assumed truth. That assumption can propagate through downstream decisions, compounding unnoticed until the software produces a wrong result, or the right result for the wrong reason.

Holonograph identifies interpretive dependencies at machine speed, shows where they influence consequential decisions, and grants authority on evidence.

The problem

A valid value can carry a wrong interpretation.

Conceptual example

A ticket reads “I hope your launch is a success.” A pattern pulls out success, maps it to route.SUCCESS, and a branch downstream closes the incident.

Every type checks. The enum is valid. The interpretation is wrong, and nothing in the test suite can see it, because the test suite checks the code.

The interpreter differs from system to system: a pattern over free text, a fuzzy match on a name, a classifier selecting a rule, a model answering a question. The handoff is the same, and once the value is a typed, validated fact, every decision after it inherits the assumption.

Authority governance

Authority, derived from the record.

Authority is the state, held per decision surface, that the declared prerequisites for a consequential transition are satisfied on the record as it stands. It is granted on evidence, re-derived at every read, and no actor confers it.

Four neighbouring words, and none of them is it.

PermissionAn actor is allowed to act.
ApprovalAn actor affirmatively accepts something.
CorrectnessA proposition matches an applicable reference.
ConfidenceA degree of belief, or a measurement's precision.
AuthorityThe declared prerequisites for this transition are satisfied on the record.

Humans, builders and mechanicals all produce events on the record. An operator approving a decision is approval, recorded as such. It does not move the state.

Six states a surface can be in.

unmappedNo decision here is known to act on an interpreted value. Not yet mapped, rather than no seam.
mappedA decision is identified. Nothing asserts authority over it.
declaredAuthority is asserted and the gate is live, but no qualifying evidence is accruing.
qualifyingLive traffic is accumulating the evidence that would establish the condition.
grantedThe condition is established on live evidence and every dependency holds its declared envelope.
suspendedA dependency left its declared envelope and the grant was withdrawn by the apparatus.

The first five are a ladder. The sixth is a withdrawal, drawn as a branch and never as a completion stage. Re-establishing a grant takes the re-satisfaction of every gate over the post-change record, not an acknowledgement.

Two things never confer authority.

An approved corrective change is a recorded change. It enters the record; it does not feed a grant. Whether delivery proceeds is operator or CI policy reading the record; the lens never blocks on its own authority.

Reflection is granted, never switched on.

When a response fails a certified judge, the judge's grounded critique can go back to the producer for a capped retry. That is granted per judge, rubric, facet and surface, only after the cell certifies, and revoked on any change to the identity it was computed on.

Reflection against an uncertified judge turns the judge into an optimization target, and the cheapest way for a producer to raise a score on a requires-confirmation dimension is to stop being honest about absence. So the loop opens only where authority already stands.

Seams

Where inference becomes a decision.

An interpreted value driving a decision that governs a consequential transition is a seam. It is permanent. You can close it and you can monitor it. You cannot validate it away.

Provenance survives every hop.

Consider a decision that reads free text and returns one of three values:

function decide(ticket): 'page' | 'auto-reply' | 'queue' {
  const msg = (ticket.subject + ticket.body).toLowerCase();   // interpreted
  if (INCIDENT.test(msg)) return 'page';
  ...
}

const action = decide(ticket);        // deterministic in value, interpreted in provenance
if (action === 'page') pageOnCall();  // the dispatch reaches authority

The enum is not a sanitizer. It is the decision's codomain. action is deterministic in what it holds and interpreted in where it came from, and the provenance rides through every downstream hop. Moving the decision upstream moves the decision point; the next scan finds it in its new place.

A schema boundary filters the catastrophic and passes the shape-valid-but-wrong. It raises the floor and, in the same motion, makes the residual error insidious: the value now looks right, types right, and fails downstream. Closing a seam makes the decision attended and monitorable. Correctness is a different axis, and the only instrument for it is observation on real inputs over time.

closedThe decision point has a defined no-match or abstention path, and its output's provenance chain ends in consumers that do not decide.
openAny unattended decision point in the chain, or a chain that feeds an unattended authority decision. The defective state.

A model is a seam whose interpreter moves.

A pattern does not rewrite itself between releases. A model can be re-routed behind an unchanged name, and the instrument scoring it is usually another model. So a model seam carries more variance control. The lifecycle is the same.

A model can sit on either side of the seam: as the interpreter whose output a branch consumes, or as the decider that consumes an interpreted input and chooses an action. Holonograph labels the interpretation, the decision it influences, and the action boundary separately, so a model occupying both roles in one workflow is read as two dependencies.

Attribution

When a score moves, investigate what moved with it.

A changed score can reflect your deployment, the model that answered, the evaluation configuration, or noise. Holonograph keeps those identities on every observation, so a reading is compared under the versions that produced it. Where the record cannot separate two explanations, the reading says so and names the observation that would.

Four sources of variance, within one immutable event.

SubstrateYour deployment: the prompts, tools, code and declared context you control.
Light sourceThe model that answered, and everything the provider controls about it.
LensThe evaluation apparatus: judges, rubrics, configurations.
NoiseResidual per-call variation, characterized as a floor a real change has to clear.

A change in the measurement apparatus can look like a change in the system being measured. Holonograph preserves the identities and relationships needed to tell them apart, across every declared dimension and every version.

Attribution requires memory.

A number can move without the behavior moving. When the lens that scores a surface is revised, the metric drifts while the behavior may not have changed at all. Holonograph versions every lens and keeps the full history, so each reading is checked against the exact lens that produced it.

The model you asked for and the model that answered are two different facts. A vendor can re-route behind an unchanged alias. Holonograph records both sides, so that change has a name instead of showing up as unexplained drift. The judge has the same two sides.

Builders

Reported in the session that wrote it.

The seam monitor runs inside the build loop. When a seam that reaches authority is written, it is reported at birth, in the session, naming the sink it reaches and the coverage the scan could not follow.

What a report says, and the choice it leaves you.

A report names the interpretation, the decision it reaches, and what the scan could not follow. The scan reports structural facts and never a verdict; a separate gate adjudicates whether the edge is a real seam, and only confirmed edges are registered. The choice is yours: declare the seam, or restructure around it. Accepted declarations are honored, in the scan and in the lens.

You are not asked to author the sink and skip lists cold. The monitor proposes candidates for both from the code it has already read.

The monitor flags and drafts. It does not edit code and it does not block a build on its own authority. Whether a build stops is operator or CI policy, and the detector qualifies before that policy is eligible: observe first, everywhere, including on its own repository.

During development, a conventional implementation review left an authority-bearing dependency unresolved. Seam analysis identified where an interpreted value was being treated as more authoritative than its provenance justified: a heuristic inference presented as established information, which filtered the rules the model was shown, and a fuzzy match carrying the same label as an exact one.

The producer's code did not need to change. The consequential behavior downstream did. Subsequent investigation identified further authority-bearing dependencies in rule selection and evaluation context.

An account from development, not an independently reproduced case study. It illustrates the gap between a correct producer and an unjustified downstream decision; it does not establish a prevented failure. A reported seam is not automatically a defect.

Research

What Does an LLM Judge Actually Measure?

The Role of Evaluation Frames in Construct Discrimination. Brian Kerrigan, 2026.

The finding.

An LLM judge can produce a coherent rationale and a stable-looking score without establishing that the score measures the defect the operator intended. Two prospectively governed studies tested controlled defects across three fixed evaluator configurations: 4,800 judgments under ordinary holistic scoring, then 8,640 across three evaluation frames.

Under ordinary holistic scoring, no configuration established the preregistered target-discrimination criterion. Under target-bound scoring, all three did. Before relying on a judge for consequential inference, its ability to discriminate the relevant construct should be demonstrated under the intended evaluation frame.

Calls passed through dedicated local Holonograph Lens instances, which supplied call-time correlation, route and served-model identity, substrate stamps, cohort tags, and immutable digest-only events. Raw requests and responses stayed outside the Lens.

Disclosure. Holonograph is developed by the study's author. The manuscript states this in its own methods, and so does this page.
