Chapter 25

Outcome delivery

The trip this chapter is about is the outcome’s trip to the user, after scoring, not the event’s trip into the lens. A surface can be green on quality and still never reach anyone, and the two facts are measured separately. The feature is called declare-your-reach, and its discipline is that the lens reports delivery only as far as it can observe it.

What it is

The contract declares the post-outcome stages an outcome traverses between the lens’s scoring point and the user. Stages fan in across surfaces, so each is declared once at the contract root and referenced by the surfaces that route through it. 

{
  "postOutcomeStages": {
    "digest-batch":  { "kind": "deferred-delivery", "observability": "declared" },
    "policy-filter": { "kind": "filter", "observability": "declared" },
    "inbox": {
      "kind": "other",
      "observability": "reconciled",
      "settleMs": 3600000
    }
  },
  "surfaces": [
    {
      "surfaceId": "draft-reply",
      "postOutcomeStages": ["digest-batch", "policy-filter", "inbox"]
    }
  ]
}

A surface with no postOutcomeStages makes no delivery claim, and the lens says nothing about its reach. A reference that does not resolve in the map is ignored by the reach reader rather than failing registration, which keeps a stale ref from taking down a contract.

KindWhat it does to an outcome
deferred-deliveryHeld for a schedule or batch window, delivered later.
filterMay suppress it. A rule can drop it before the user.
cacheServed from or written to a cache layer.
rate-limitThrottled. May coalesce or shed under load.
fan-inMerges outcomes from several surfaces into one delivery.
otherDeclared and uncategorized.

filter is the only kind that can suppress an outcome. The rest delay it. The distinction changes what a verdict on the surface is worth, which is why the kind is declared rather than inferred.

Each stage also declares whether the lens can see traversal. declared means the operator is telling the lens the stage exists while it emits nothing: the lens cannot verify delivery through it, so every verdict on that surface covers scoring only, and the read reports that. reconciled means the stage emits a footprint per outcome, so the lens observes traversal and derives each outcome’s fate.

The lens never accepts a reported disposition An operator may not tell the lens that an outcome was delivered. A delivery pipeline broken enough to drop outcomes is exactly the pipeline whose self-report cannot be trusted. The operator declares the topology, which is static, and emits observations of traversal. The verdict on each outcome is derived, and a scored outcome with no terminal footprint past its settle window is read off the absence: the pipeline that drops the outcome drops its footprint too.

Why it matters

A verdict is a claim about what the agent produced. Treating it as a claim about what the user received is the substitution this chapter exists to block. On a surface with a declared filter downstream, a clean verdict and a user who never saw the output are fully compatible, and nothing in the scoring record would show it.

So the attention spine carries both halves of the finding under one source, reach-gap, with different weight. The horizon variant is a declared scope limit rather than a detected failure, so it rides the quiet coverage band. The shortfall variant is a detected failure, with severity scaled to the observed dark rate.

How to use it

Emit footprints

POST /lens/reach/footprint
{
  "correlationId": "corr_01H...",
  "surfaceId": "draft-reply",
  "stageId": "inbox",
  "occurredAt": "2026-11-06T18:22:14.812Z"
}

One observation: the outcome for this correlation id was seen reaching this stage at this time. Nothing about whether that was good. 202 with the count accepted, and the body also takes { "footprints": [ ... ] } for the high-volume path.

The store dedups by correlation id and stage, keep-first, so retries are safe. The strongest footprint is the user’s own return, a reply or a read receipt, emitted on the terminal stage. Without a footprint store wired, or without a contract registry to scope the write, the endpoint answers 501.

Read the rate

GET /lens/reach?surfaceId=draft-reply&since=2026-10-01T00:00:00Z

One entry per surface that declares stages. Surfaces that make no claim are omitted rather than reported empty. Each entry names the terminal stage and its kind, and classifies the read:

StateMeaningReport
measuredThe terminal stage is reconciled and outcomes have settled.Present.
horizonThe terminal stage is declared. The lens is told it exists and cannot observe it.null. No rate is derived.
no-outcomesReconciled, but nothing was scored on the surface in the window.null.

Omitting since applies the operator’s default read window, echoed back as readWindow so the denominator is never implicit.

The five dispositions

DispositionDerived from
in-flightScored, no terminal footprint, still within the settle window. An honest unknown.
deliveredA footprint at the terminal reconciled stage.
darkPast the settle window with no terminal footprint. Observed absence.
supersededA newer outcome for the same target replaced it.
expiredA declared freshness deadline passed before delivery.

The first three are derived today. The last two are declared in the enum so the vocabulary is whole and the fuller model arrives without a wire change. settleMs on the terminal stage sets the boundary between in-flight and dark; absent, the reader’s default applies.

Reading the output

darkRate is dark / settled, where settled is delivered + dark. In-flight outcomes are excluded from both sides, so an outcome whose fate is still unknown is never counted as either. With nothing settled the rate is null rather than zero.

byLastStage groups the dark outcomes by the furthest stage a footprint was seen at, which is the useful read: where they stopped. A stageId of null means the outcome was never seen past scoring. medianDarkAgeMs says how old the dark ones are, which separates a broken stage from a slow one.

The reach-gap exemplar in the spine carries the declared topology in order, each stage with its kind and observability, the scored-outcome count as the denominator, and a flag when a suppressing stage sits among the unobserved ones. For the horizon variant the sample list is empty by construction: the proof is the declaration, not a representative call.

Where footprints live The footprint store is append-only and separate from the canonical event store, and it is never an input to the lens hash. Footprints are transient observations, so a backend may fold settled outcomes into per-surface, per-day tallies and prune past the rate window. The derived rate is what persists. Growth is bounded by the in-flight outcomes plus surfaces times days in window, never by every footprint ever emitted.

Where this sits

Sidecar / OTel integration covers the other direction: the event’s trip into the lens, and what happens when a drain channel is unreachable. The attention spine carries the reach-gap source and its two bands. The Trust tab in The dashboard renders this read beside rubric coverage, since both answer whether a green verdict can be believed.

The stage vocabulary, the disposition enum, the footprint shape and the report shape are public contract. The pairing, the disposition derivation and the rate aggregation live in the lens.
