Chapter 17

Judge modes and reflection

Every judge/gate relationship on a surface is one of four modes, the valid cells of two independent questions: does the producer wait for the verdict, and may the verdict touch delivery. Reflection is the third cell, and the only one Holonograph grants rather than leaving to configuration.

What it is

Two axes. Timing is async (the judge runs off the producer's hot path) or inline (the producer waits for the verdict before finishing). Authority over delivery runs open (observe only) to reflection (the judge may send the producer back to retry) to gate (delivery is blocked).

asyncOpen, asynchronousimpossibleimpossible
inlineOpen, inlineReflection grantedActually gated

The two empty cells are impossible rather than unspecified. Reflection and gating both need the verdict before the producer finishes, and an asynchronous verdict is not back yet. The consequence is worth stating plainly: async is available only to a judge that observes and nothing more.

Why it matters

The judge never disposes

Across all four modes the judge's authority stops at reflection. It can observe, and at most it can send the producer back to try again. It cannot block delivery. Disposal belongs to deterministic code in the gate, which is producer-owned, or to operator policy. Propose-only holds in every mode.

Inline and observing is the expensive cell

A judge that runs in-request while its verdict touches nothing pays full latency for no in-band effect. A fleet of observe-only judges parked there is where large latency overheads come from. The cell is justified as a step toward reflection or a gate, not as a destination. Move the judge to async, or give it authority.

How to use it

  • Pick the mode per surface. A judge that only records belongs in async.
  • Reflection is a capped retry, not a block. Budget the retries.
  • A gate is deterministic code reading a verdict. Write the gate; do not ask the model to be one.

Reading the output

An asynchronous verdict lands after the producer has answered, as a linked judge event. A reader resolves it link-first and falls back to the embedded verdict, fail-open, so a consumer never blocks on a verdict that has not arrived. Per-judgment verdict provenance distinguishes the raw judge result from the producer-filtered one.

Why reflection is gated

Reflection turns a judge from an evaluator you read into an optimization target the producer chases. That inverts the trust requirement. A judge you only read can be a little wrong, because you flag and a human reviews. A judge you optimize against has to be right, because the producer will find and exploit every gap in it.

The gate is on validity, not on discrimination. Those come apart: a judge can score stably and still be blind, saturated, or wrong about how a construct should treat absence. Scoring the same way every time is reliability, and reliability does not imply validity.

The specific hazard

Real dimensions are often requires-confirmation: an honest "I do not have the data" correctly scores low. Put that judge in a retry loop and the cheapest way for the producer to raise its score is to stop being honest about the absence and fabricate. That is the exact failure the apparatus exists to catch, now with a loop optimizing toward it.

So reflection without established authority is not merely a waste of tokens and time. It is a pressure applied to the one behavior you most need preserved.

What has to hold before a cell reflects

Reflection unlocks for a judge × rubric × facet × surface cell when all of the following hold. The gate is dual: Holonograph computes the statistical pass, then a human confirms.

  • Soak. Enough live production data to reach a statistical N. Holonograph determines the statistical pass; a human approves the N, judging whether the sample spans the breadth of real inputs and outputs.
  • Discrimination. The facet's dimensions pass discrimination evaluation, with a human confirming key outlier calls and a statistical sampling of calls on that facet.
  • Saturation cleared. Every dimension passes the saturation audit, which is a read you can run yourself.

Validity is cell-specific, so the grant is too. A judge certified on one facet of one surface has said nothing about another.

What reflection does

A failing response returns to the producer with the judge's grounded critique attached, and the producer retries, capped. Reflection is the soft tier of the judge's authority over delivery: it can send the producer back, it cannot block. Blocking remains deterministic code in the gate, or operator policy.

Revocation

The grant is revoked on any change to the identity it was computed on. A new judge model, a revised rubric, a changed context scope: the cell certifies again or it does not reflect. This is the authority machinery applied to the judge rather than to the producer, and it suspends the same way for the same reasons.

The shared-frame check

One of those preconditions has an instrument of its own. Groundedness has to hold on both sides: a judge that read a different frame from the producer is not measuring the producer, and a groundedness alarm on such a pair points at the wrong party.

Over the surface's claim-instrumented judged dimensions it classifies each judge-to-subject frame relation:

RelationWhat it says
shared-frame-intactJudge and producer were grounded in the same values. A groundedness finding here is about the producer.
judge-misread-frameThe judge read the frame wrong. The finding is about the judge.
judge-frame-truncatedThe judge saw part of the frame. Its verdict is scoped to what it saw, whatever it asserts.
judge-grounded-outside-frameThe judge grounded on something the producer never had. It is marking the producer down for an absence that was never the producer's.
not-assessableThe relation could not be established. Reported rather than assumed intact.

The last two are the observer-groundedness asymmetry, and they are the disambiguation to run before treating a groundedness alarm as a producer fault. Deterministic per verdict, certain or abstain: no false-discovery gate and no data floor, because each relation is established or it is not. Read-only, so it proposes and the operator disposes.

Your own reflection loop remains yours to run; nothing here prevents it. The claim is narrower: a retry loop against an uncertified judge spends tokens to apply pressure in an unknown direction. Holonograph turns reflection on when the cell has qualified, if you have asked for it.

The taxonomy is the design of record for the judge/gate relationship and is documented ahead of the implementation. The reflection path and the judge-gate enforcement cluster each have their own specification; this chapter is the frame that places them.
