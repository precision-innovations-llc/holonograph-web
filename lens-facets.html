Lens facets

When a scale stops discriminating

When one judge scores many concerns at once, scores can concentrate at an anchor: a ten and a nine stop being different readings, and a failure shows only that something went wrong. Holonograph reports that as a candidate on the event and names the measurement that would confirm it.

Facets of rubric, judge, and context scope

The condition

Scores concentrated at an anchor

Ask one judge to score correctness, tone, concision, and safety in a single number and scores can concentrate at an anchor, the top or the floor of the scale. Concentration alone is a candidate, not a verdict: the scale may have stopped discriminating, or the reading may be correct and stable there. The measurement that separates them is whether distinct subject states land on the same anchor. A scale that fails that measurement is saturated.

Scores at an anchor

One anchor value. The number shows no cause.

Resolved into facets

Facets of rubric, judge, and context scope. Each is grouped and read on its own scale.

The detection

Holonograph reports the candidate

The lens detects it. A check reads whether scores have concentrated at an anchor and reports a candidate on that event. It runs at window scope on the reliability read and returns a machine-readable signal a coding agent can check before ranking on a graded score. The check runs over data already captured, so it makes no fresh model call and returns the same result every time.

Facets

Attribution by facet

A rubric can resolve into facets of rubric, judge, and context scope. Holonograph groups and attributes each one, so a reading is tied to the facet it belongs to.

01

Report

The candidate appears on the event as a readable signal.

02

Attribute

Each declared facet is grouped and read on its own scale.

03

Mark

Where two facets change together, Holonograph marks the reading as confounded.

Why it matters

Readings you can act on

A saturated score looks graded and carries almost no information. A coding agent that checks the signal first can rank by reliability tier instead of score magnitude.
